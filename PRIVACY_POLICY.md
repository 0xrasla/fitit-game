# Privacy Policy for Inkstinct

Last updated: June 13, 2026

## Overview

Inkstinct ("we", "our", or "the app") is a free, ad-supported mobile game. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

## Information We Collect

### Ad Services
Inkstinct uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob may collect and use device information, including:

- Device advertising ID (e.g., Google Advertising ID)
- Device type and operating system version
- General location (not precise GPS)
- App usage data for ad personalization

For more details, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Local Storage
The app stores the following data locally on your device using SharedPreferences:
- Game high scores
- Sound and haptic settings
- Games played count

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

## No User Accounts

Inkstinct does **not** require or support user accounts. We do not collect names, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information.

## Third-Party Services

**Google AdMob** — Used to display advertisements. You can read AdMob's privacy policy at: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

## Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data Deletion

Since all game data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all data by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the app.

## Changes

We may update this privacy policy. Changes will be posted here with an updated date.
